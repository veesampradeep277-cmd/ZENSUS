What Are Operators?

Operators are symbols or keywords used to perform operations on values.

Think of operators as instructions

In Python, an operator tells the computer what to do with one or more values. For example, + adds values, > compares values, and and combines conditions.

Operator Precedence

Determines which operation Python performs first.

Why does precedence matter?

When an expression contains multiple operators, Python follows a specific order to evaluate them.

10 + 5 × 2
5 × 2 = 10
→
10 + 10 = 20
→
Answer = 20
Python evaluates multiplication before addition.

Important Precedence Order

1 () Parentheses
2 ** Exponentiation
3 +x -x ~x Unary operators
4 * / // % @ Multiplication, division, floor division, modulus and matrix multiplication
5 + - Addition and subtraction
6 << >> Bit shifts
7 & Bitwise AND
8 ^ Bitwise XOR
9 | Bitwise OR
10 == != > < >= <= Comparisons
11 not Logical NOT
12 and Logical AND
13 or Logical OR
Python PY 
result = 10 + 5 * 2

print(result)
Output: 20
